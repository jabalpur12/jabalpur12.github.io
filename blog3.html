Many people wonder whether RO water is safe for daily drinking. With increasing water pollution, RO purifiers have become a necessity in most homes.

💧 What is RO Water?

RO (Reverse Osmosis) is a filtration process that removes impurities, harmful chemicals, bacteria, and heavy metals from water.

✅ Is RO Water Safe?

Yes, RO water is completely safe when your purifier is properly maintained. It provides clean and purified drinking water.

⚠️ Common Myths

Some people believe RO removes all minerals. However, modern systems like Merlin RO maintain essential minerals for healthy drinking water.

🔧 Why Maintenance is Important

  • Ensures proper filtration
  • Keeps water taste fresh
  • Prevents bacteria buildup
  • Improves purifier life

"Safe drinking water is essential for a healthy life. Regular RO service ensures purity."
